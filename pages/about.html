← Back to Home

This platform helps visualize and track civic and political events across Vienna. Users can submit protests, workshops, elections, and other forms of participation using a user-friendly interface combining calendar and map. Its purpose is to help connect local communities and show people ways to get involved in shaping the political landscape of the city of Vienna through initiatives and events. There is a database in the form of a GeoJSON file. The events come from many local initiatives and datasets from the city of Vienna, which are available under a Creative Commons license. Additionally, events can be filtered by type (category) and by whether the event repeats (daily, weekly, monthly, etc.). After filtering, events are displayed in the calendar and on the map.

Features

  • Interactive map with categorized events
  • Calendar synchronized with filters
  • Moderated user-submitted events
  • Fully responsive design
  • Login and profile management
