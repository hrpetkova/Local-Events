This platform helps visualize and track civic and political events across Vienna. Users can submit protests, workshops, elections, and other forms of participation using a user-friendly interface combining calendar and map.

Why Civic Events Matter

Civic engagement is a cornerstone of a healthy democracy. By collecting and sharing data on political events, we raise awareness, foster participation, and empower communities. This platform helps users discover local events and participate in decision-making processes.

Features

  • 🗺️ Interactive map with categorized events
  • 📆 Calendar synchronized with filters
  • 📤 Moderated user-submitted events
  • 📱 Fully responsive design
  • 🔐 Login and profile management
