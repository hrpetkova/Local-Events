This platform was created as part of the Multimedia Cartography & Web Mapping course at TU Wien. Our idea was simple: make it easier for people in Vienna to find out what's happening around them when it comes to civic and political events. Whether it's a protest, a town hall meeting, or an educational workshop, we wanted to bring it all together on one map - with a calendar to match.

We also thought it was important to let the community participate directly. That's why we added a form where people can submit their own events or initiatives. In a city full of action and ideas, it just makes sense to give everyone a way to be part of the platform.

What We Wanted to Achieve

Our main goal was to support civic engagement in a way that feels intuitive and useful. We wanted to build a digital space where people can stay informed, get involved, and connect with others who care about similar things. By putting events on a map and in a calendar, we hoped to make public life just a bit more visible - and accessible.

How It Works

The core of the platform is a map and a calendar. Both are fully interactive and work together, so when you filter by date or category, you get a clearer picture of what's going on. You can browse upcoming events, submit your own, or just explore. We also added a review system so everything that gets published is checked first - just to keep things clean and relevant.

What's Under the Hood

We built the platform using standard web technologies: HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. For the map, we used Leaflet; for the calendar, FullCalendar. The site is responsive, so it works across devices, and we wrote the code in a way that's easy to maintain and build on later. The goal was always to keep things simple, solid, and open to improvement.

Where We'd Like to Go Next

There's still a lot of room to grow. In future versions, we'd like to include real-time event updates, support for multiple languages, and better search and filter tools. We also see potential in letting organizations submit events in bulk, and maybe even sending users reminders about things they're interested in. There's plenty left to do - but this is a start.
